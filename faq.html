Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about JoinDev. Still stuck? Join our Discord and ask away.

How do I start?

Click this — the OAuth2 will auto-redirect you, and when the bot is authorized it will send you a help DM with everything you need to get going.

How will I know what servers the bot joins me to?

Discord's official bot will message you. You'll receive a DM every time JoinDev adds you to a server, so you always know exactly where you've been placed.

How many days until I get my JoinCoin for being in a server?

Three full days. You must remain in the server for a complete 72 hours before the JoinCoin is credited to your account.

Am I able to purchase JoinCoins?

Yes, but not yet. JoinDev is a free service at its core, but purchasing JoinCoins will be an optional feature in the future for users who want to accelerate their growth.

Why does the bot need administrator?

The bot needs administrator because it requires Manage Server permissions and the ability to create invites in order to function. The OAuth2 permission flow was also being finicky, so administrator ended up being the simplest reliable option. 😂

What happens if the bot token gets leaked?

The bot will automatically put itself into a shutdown state. This is a built-in safety measure so that a compromised token can't be abused — the service goes offline until the issue is resolved.

What's the status of the service?

The current status is beta. Features may change, and you might occasionally run into rough edges — but the core experience is live and working.

How do JoinCoins work?

You gain 1 JoinCoin for every server you stay in for more than three days that the bot adds you to. Stay active, stay in servers, and your coin balance grows.

⚠️
General note

If the bot starts adding you to servers even though you have no active farm JoinCoins requests, that is a malfunction. Please immediately run /cancel_joinr to stop it.
