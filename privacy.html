Privacy Policy

What data we collect, why, and how to remove it.

Last updated: September 29, 2025

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data JoinDev collects when you authorize our Discord bot, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and how you can request its removal.

By authorizing JoinDev, you consent to the data practices described here.

2. What Data We Collect

When you authorize JoinDev, we collect the following information:

Data Type Why We Collect It
Discord User ID To identify your account, track JoinCoins, and manage orders
Discord Username To display your name in DMs and admin tools
OAuth2 Access & Refresh Tokens To add you to servers on your behalf (the guilds.join scope)
IP Address Alt detection — to catch users who abuse the system with multiple accounts
JoinCoin Balance To track your earnings and spending
Order History To process and complete your server growth orders
Timestamps To enforce daily cooldowns and 3-day reward windows
✅
What we do NOT collect

We never read your messages, DMs, or server content. We don't know what servers you're in beyond what's needed to add you. We don't collect your email, real name, or location.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only for:

  • Running the JoinDev bot and its commands
  • Adding you to servers when orders are placed
  • Tracking your JoinCoins, daily streaks, and order history
  • Detecting abuse and alting through IP comparison
  • Sending you DMs about your orders and rewards

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

4. IP Address & Alt Detection

JoinDev stores your IP address when you authorize via OAuth. This is used exclusively for our Dev-Anti-Alt system to detect users who create multiple accounts to exploit JoinCoins.

  • IPs are stored for a maximum of 90 days after your last activity
  • IPs are only accessed by the bot administrators
  • IPs are never sold, shared, or used for advertising
  • Your IP is deleted when you unauthorize the bot (see Section 7)
ℹ️
Why we need this

Without IP-based detection, anyone could farm hundreds of JoinCoins with alt accounts, draining the economy and making the service unfair for real users.

5. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as you use JoinDev:

  • Active users: Data retained while you're authorized
  • IP addresses: Retained up to 90 days after your last activity
  • Inactive users (no activity for 1 year): Data may be purged automatically
  • After unauthorization: Deleted within 30 days
  • Banned users: Data retained for 1 year for appeal purposes, then deleted

6. Data Security

Your data is stored on a secured PostgreSQL database hosted on Render. Access is restricted to the JoinDev bot administrators. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest.

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure. If a security incident affects your data, we will notify affected users via Discord DM.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

7.1 Access Your Data

You can see what JoinDev stores about you by running /status in Discord. For a full data export, open a support ticket.

7.2 Delete Your Data

To have all your data deleted:

  • Option 1: Unauthorize JoinDev in your Discord settings (User Settings → Authorized Apps → JoinDev → Deauthorize)
  • Option 2: Open a support ticket requesting data deletion
  • Option 3: DM the bot /delete_my_data (if available)

Once unauthorized, your data will be deleted within 30 days.

7.3 Correct Your Data

If any of your stored data is incorrect (e.g., wrong coin balance), open a support ticket.

7.4 Object or Restrict Processing

You can leave the farming pool at any time with /cancel_joinr. This stops future processing of your account for server joins.

8. Children's Privacy

JoinDev is not intended for users under 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover we have, we will delete it immediately.

9. Third-Party Services

JoinDev relies on the following third-party services:

  • Discord — for OAuth2, bot hosting, and user data source
  • Render — for hosting the bot and PostgreSQL database
  • GitHub Pages — for hosting this website

Each service has its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing them if you have concerns.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Significant changes will be announced in our support server. Continued use of JoinDev after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or reports, open a ticket in our support server: discord.gg/HJbbKYbr2y
